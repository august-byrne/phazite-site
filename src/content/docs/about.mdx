Phazite is a free, open-source digital audio workstation for making beats and finishing songs, built in the open by the Phazite contributors. It is written in C++20 on [JUCE](https://juce.com), with a playback engine of its own, for Windows first and then Linux and macOS.

## What it aims for

- **One track, one channel.** Every track is a row in the playlist, a chain of devices and a mixer channel, made together with one name and one color. There is no channel rack.
- **One window, with no beginner or pro mode.** Depth shows where you are: in the inspector, the menus and the command search.
- **Every action is a command,** with a name, a sentence that says what it does, and keys you can change.
- **Help where you are:** the bar along the bottom says what the mouse and the keys will do under the pointer.
- **Nothing hidden:** routing, linked clips, automation and modulation show where they act.
- **Stable and fast:** Auto Save, recovery after a crash, plug-ins in processes of their own, and strict real-time rules in its audio engine.
- **Local and private:** no account, and your projects stay on your computer.

[The design](https://github.com/august-byrne/Phazite/blob/main/docs/design.md) explains each choice and the research behind it, [the roadmap](https://github.com/august-byrne/Phazite/blob/main/docs/roadmap.md) has the plan step by step, and [the project board](https://github.com/users/august-byrne/projects/1) shows what comes next, by month.
